Intro[MBFC.1]

82% of the marine biodiversity footprint of sea freight is missed by carbon accounting: ballast water, underwater noise, whale strikes, scrubber discharges. While 80% of world trade by volume goes by sea. The MBFC measures it route by route, turns it into biodiversity criteria for your carriers and forwarders, and into a reporting and communication kit.
