A reliable, auditable method

The calculation

  • Magnitude = intensity × (one + extent × frequency × reversibility × probability), weighted by your client’s routes and volumes (TEU or tonnes).
  • Residual impact = magnitude × (one − reduction achieved by their carriers' actions).
  • 13 pages of calculations, summarised in a clear, useful interface.

A hybrid approach

  • Qualitative: 50+ scientific papers aggregated.
  • Quantitative: 15+ databases, plus six data points from your client.

High confidence

  • 90–100% on the completeness of the pressures identified
  • 66–100% on their weighting to the client’s context
  • Frameworks: SBTN, TNFD, CSRD (ESRS E4), European Commission
  • Data: Copernicus Marine, GBIF, IBAT, Protected Planet, UNEP-WCMC

A continuously improved model. Every figure is sourced and auditable.
