Your goods travel by sea: good news for the climate. But the ships that carry them also move invasive species, make underwater noise, cross paths with whales and discharge scrubber washwater. Together that is 82% of the marine biodiversity footprint of sea freight, and carbon accounting misses it. You don’t own the ships. But you are the one who chooses them.

  • Compute your sea freight’s impact on marine biodiversity. Choose your carriers accordingly. Communicate.

  • Three weeks, three hours of your time, €9,000 ex. VAT, turnkey. Aligned with SBTN, TNFD and CSRD (ESRS E4).

What measurement changed[MBFC.2]

What measurement changed

12 priority pressures identified out of 22. Without action, +20% footprint in six years as freight shifts to sea. With the specifications, up to −90% by 2030, and 10 biodiversity criteria written into the freight tenders.

You offer the study, we deliver it in 3 weeks, you hand over the three deliverables under your brand or co-branded. Fixed partner price: €9,000 ex. VAT per study, resold at the price you set. The annual update becomes a recurring assignment for you.

Our reference shipper client has already built 10 biodiversity criteria into its freight tenders. Actions on routing, speed, hulls, ballast and noise can cut up to 70% of the estimated impact with no significant extra cost.

50+ scientific papers, 15+ databases, 13 pages of calculation. 90–100% confidence on the completeness of the pressures identified, 66–100% on their weighting to your context. Every figure is sourced and auditable.
